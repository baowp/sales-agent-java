# Retail returns & refunds (U.S. consumer orders only)
Source: https://www.sockspal.com/returns (version 2026-08-29). Wholesale and custom claims follow the wholesale terms.

- Retail goods are final sale: no returns for change of mind, wrong size or colour chosen, style or fit.
- Exceptions (qualifying problems): manufacturing defect on delivery, holes or failed seams not caused by use, contamination, significant transit damage, item materially different from the description, or wrong product/size/colour/quantity sent. Normal wear, misuse, improper care and on-screen colour differences do not qualify.
- Report within 30 calendar days of recorded delivery (hidden defects: promptly after discovery). Email sockspal@163.com with the order number, item, description, discovery date and clear photos of the item, packaging and label. Keep the item until instructed.
- Remedy: replacement, full or partial refund, or another legal remedy. No restocking fee. If a return is needed, SocksPal provides prepaid postage or reimburses authorised postage.
- Approved refunds go to the original PayPal payment within 10 business days after confirmation (or after the authorised return is inspected).
- Unpaid orders can be cancelled from the order page; for paid orders not yet dispatched, contact SocksPal immediately.
