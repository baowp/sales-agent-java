# International Wholesale Terms of Sale (summary)
Source: https://www.sockspal.com/wholesale-terms (version 2026-08-29). This is a summary; the full page is binding.

- Scope: business buyers only (trade, resale, professional use). Consumers follow the U.S. consumer terms.
- Binding contract: an RFQ, product page, sample discussion or estimate is not an order. A contract forms only when SocksPal issues a written order confirmation after unqualified acceptance and any required deposit. Quotations expire on their stated validity date.
- MOQ and specs: MOQs apply as quoted. The quotation and the approved specification define the goods. No overrun/underrun tolerance unless stated. Screen and sample colours may vary within agreed tolerance.
- Samples and artwork: sample, tooling and development fees are in the quotation and are non-refundable once work starts. Approving a sample or proof authorises production. The buyer keeps ownership of its logos and grants a licence only to make the order.
- Price and payment: currency, unit prices, shipping charges and payment schedule are in the quotation. Prices exclude destination duties, import taxes, VAT/GST, bank fees and buyer-side clearance costs unless stated. Overdue amounts may accrue interest (lower of 1.5%/month or the legal maximum).
- Changes and cancellation: changes after confirmation need written agreement. A confirmed order can be cancelled only with SocksPal's written agreement; the buyer pays for completed work and committed costs (capped at the order price).
- Lead time: starts after the deposit, final specification and artwork approval. It is an estimate unless the confirmation guarantees it.
- Delivery: quotation states the Incoterm (Incoterms 2020) and named place. FOB/CIF for sea only; FCA for container/multimodal; DAP buyer clears import; DDP seller clears import. Title passes after full payment.
- Claims: report visible shortages, transit damage or non-conformity within 30 calendar days of delivery; latent manufacturing defects promptly and within 6 months. Remedies: repair, replace, refund or credit. No returns without written authorisation. No returns for change of mind, slow resale, excess stock or buyer ordering errors.
- Compliance: buyer is responsible for destination-market labelling, fibre content, safety, packaging and import rules unless the quotation says otherwise.
- Liability is capped at the amount of the affected order; no indirect or consequential loss.
- Law: People's Republic of China (CISG excluded); courts of Hangzhou, Zhejiang.
