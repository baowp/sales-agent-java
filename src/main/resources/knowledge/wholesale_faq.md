# Wholesale & custom orders: FAQ and process
Source: https://www.sockspal.com/ (FAQ) and https://www.sockspal.com/custom-socks

## Who we serve
- Wholesale (buying for resale or distribution) and bulk orders (teams, events, employees, studios, promotions).
- Custom socks for sports teams, fitness studios, events, retailers and distributors.

## Custom options
- Custom colours and patterns, logo treatment (knitted, printed or grip logo, depending on construction), size assortments.
- Retail packaging: labels, bands, barcodes, individual packing.
- Artwork files are collected after the RFQ; the customer can describe the design in the RFQ first.

## What a quote needs
Sock type or product, estimated quantity in pairs, destination, intended use, sizes, colours, branding/logo, packaging, and desired timing. Missing details can be confirmed before the quotation.

## MOQ
Depends on the product and specification. Each wholesale product lists its current MOQ; the final quantity is confirmed in the quotation.

## Process
1. Customer shares a brief (RFQ).
2. SocksPal sends a quotation confirming price, MOQ, production lead time, Incoterm and named place.
3. Customer approves samples or artwork; that approval defines what is produced.
4. Production, inspection, packing, and handover under the agreed Incoterm.

## Delivery terms (Incoterms) for wholesale
- FCA: goods handed to the buyer's nominated carrier at an agreed place. For buyers with their own freight network.
- FOB: sea freight; SocksPal loads the goods onto the vessel at the agreed port; buyer controls ocean freight.
- DAP: SocksPal arranges transport to the destination; buyer handles import clearance, duties and local taxes.
Freight cost is not published. It is quoted per order in the quotation.
