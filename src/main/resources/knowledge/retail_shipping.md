# Retail shipping & DDP policy (U.S. consumer orders only)
Source: https://www.sockspal.com/shipping (version 2026-08-29). Wholesale delivery follows the quotation and Incoterm instead.

- Retail orders ship only to U.S. addresses (residential or business). Wholesale/resale purchases are excluded.
- No separate shipping fee for retail orders.
- Shipped DDP: SocksPal pays international carriage, export/import clearance, customs duties and import taxes. Customers should email sockspal@163.com before paying any unexpected carrier or customs charge.
- Dispatch: within 30 days after payment unless the product or order says otherwise. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed. If shipping is delayed, the customer is told the new date and can accept or cancel for a full refund.
- Tracking is provided through the order record or email when available. Report missing or damaged parcels promptly with the order number and photos; loss or damage before delivery gets a replacement or refund.
- Risk passes on delivery to the order address.
